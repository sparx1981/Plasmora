Welcome to Plasmora, a music visualiser for Android.

- 90+ live effects: plasma, tunnels, smoke, glass, LED and 3D scenes
- Reacts to music from YouTube Music, Spotify and more
- Like or dislike effects to shape your own playlist of visuals
- Song title, album art and lyrics on screen
- Colour, motion, glow and zoom controls
- Live wallpaper, always-on mode and picture-in-picture
- Quick intro on first launch

Requires Android 10 or newer.

PLAY STORE RELEASE NOTES (what's new) - limit 500 characters
=====
New: Voice assistant button. Tap the mic icon to ask your phone's assistant (such as Gemini) to play a different track, hands-free. Handy when driving. Turn it on or off in Settings > Player controls.

New: The Dancer progress bar has 8 dancers (adding a rocker, a latin dancer and a country dancer), each with 8 moves. They match the artist's genre and move to the energy of the music.
